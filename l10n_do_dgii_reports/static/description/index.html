DGII Reports

Comprehensive Fiscal Reports for Dominican Republic Internal Revenue Service

Overview

DGII Reports extends the functionalities of l10n_do_ncf, integrating fiscal declaration reports for compliance with Dominican Republic Internal Revenue Service (DGII) requirements. Generate all required monthly and annual reports in the exact format required by DGII.

Supported Reports

606 Report - Purchases

Generate the monthly 606 report for purchases and external services. This report includes all purchase transactions with their corresponding NCF numbers, supplier information, and tax details. The module automatically classifies transactions and applies the correct tax treatment.

Export the report in the official DGII format (TXT/XML) ready for submission through the DGII online portal.

607 Report - Sales

Comprehensive sales reporting with the 607 report that includes all sales transactions, customer information, NCF details, and tax calculations. The report automatically categorizes sales by customer type and applies proper fiscal classification.

Includes validation to ensure all sales have valid NCF numbers and proper customer classification before report generation.

608 Report - Cancellations

Track and report all invoice cancellations through the 608 report. This report captures cancelled invoices, credit notes, and other document modifications required by DGII regulations.

Automatic detection of cancelled transactions and proper categorization ensures complete compliance with DGII reporting requirements.

609 Report - External Payments

Report external payments and foreign transactions with the 609 report. This includes payments to foreign suppliers, import transactions, and other international business operations that require special fiscal treatment.

Automatic currency conversion and proper tax calculation for international transactions ensure accurate reporting of foreign operations.

IT-1 Report - Income Tax

Generate the IT-1 monthly income tax report with detailed breakdown of sales, purchases, and tax calculations. This comprehensive report includes all fiscal data required for monthly income tax declarations.

Automatic calculation of tax bases, credits, and withholdings provides accurate data for tax compliance and reduces manual calculation errors.

Report Generation Wizard

Use the comprehensive report generation wizard to create any DGII report. Select the report type, period, and additional filters to generate exactly the data you need. The wizard includes validation checks to ensure data integrity before report generation.

Access the report wizard from Accounting > Reporting > DGII Reports and follow the step-by-step process to generate professional reports.

Data Validation & Compliance

Built-in validation ensures all data meets DGII requirements before report generation. The module checks for missing NCF numbers, invalid customer classifications, incorrect tax calculations, and other compliance issues.

Comprehensive error reporting helps identify and resolve data issues before submitting reports to DGII, reducing the risk of rejections and penalties.

Export Formats

Export reports in multiple formats including the official DGII TXT format, XML for electronic submission, and Excel for analysis and review. All export formats comply with DGII specifications and can be directly uploaded to the DGII portal.

Preview reports before export to verify data accuracy and completeness. The module includes format validation to ensure files meet DGII technical requirements.
